# Human Review Guide

The purpose of human review is to turn research into a responsible business-development decision.

## Before contacting a prospect

Ask:

1. Is the business actually in the target area?
2. Is local/independent ownership supported by evidence if that is a requirement?
3. Is the business contact information current and public?
4. Is the decision-maker verified, or does the contact need to be identified first?
5. Did the research find an existing student offer?
6. Is the proposed Movement opportunity different from an existing offer when appropriate?
7. Are there any conflicting sources that need to be resolved?
8. Does the outreach angle accurately reflect the research?

## Human decisions

The reviewer may move a prospect to:

- **Ready to Contact** — sufficient information to begin outreach
- **Needs Research / Researching** — important information remains unresolved
- **Not Interested** — determined after human interaction
- **Interested** — business has expressed interest
- **Signed Up** — business has agreed to participate
- **Follow Up** — human follow-up is required

## Principle

The AI can reduce research time, but the human remains accountable for the business relationship and final decision.
