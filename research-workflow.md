# Research Workflow

## Workflow

```text
SEARCH
  ↓
VERIFY
  ↓
RESEARCH CONTACT
  ↓
CHECK EXISTING STUDENT OFFER
  ↓
IDENTIFY MOVEMENT OPPORTUNITY
  ↓
CREATE OUTREACH ANGLE
  ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
  ↓
OUTREACH
```

## Search

Define the target institution, audience, category, geographic area, desired number of prospects, and exclusions before searching.

## Verify

For each candidate, verify as many of the following as possible:

- Current business name
- Current address
- Business category
- Public phone
- Public business email or contact page
- Local/independent ownership evidence
- Current business website
- Decision-maker or partnership contact

## Contact research

Use a source hierarchy:

1. Current official business website
2. Current chamber or business directory
3. Recent reputable local reporting
4. Older sources, clearly labeled as older

If evidence conflicts, preserve the conflict.

## Student-offer research

Search the official business website first, then reputable current sources. Capture:

- Offer amount or description
- Eligibility
- Restrictions
- Current dates, if applicable
- Source

General specials should remain classified as general promotions.

## Opportunity analysis

The agent should answer:

> Why might this business care about reaching this student population?

The answer should be based on observable business characteristics, not assumptions about revenue or willingness to participate.

## Human review

The human reviewer decides whether the prospect is:

- Researching
- Ready to Contact
- Contacted
- Interested
- Signed Up
- Not Interested
- Follow Up

The AI does not change a prospect to an outreach status merely because it found a business.
